Notes

  • All stations listed are major ASOS sites with 1-minute automated observations available through IEM.
  • IEM ASOS data has a 1-4 hour lag; hourly reports only capture precip in 1-hour buckets.
  • Special observations (speci) improve timing resolution but the backtest currently skips them (report_type=1 only).
  • For 25+ cities, the collection scheduler's forecast-based filtering will keep API usage low by skipping clear-sky cities.
  • Summer (May-Sep): Southeast and Plains cities will dominate data volume. Winter (Nov-Mar): Northeast and Lakes cities will.
